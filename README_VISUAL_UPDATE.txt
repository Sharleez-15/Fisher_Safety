FISHER SAFETY — BIG ICONS & ANIMATIONS

This is the updated visual build. It includes larger weather illustrations, animated visibility fog and boat, animated wind and gusts, a pressure gauge, rotating wind direction arrow, animated weather scene, larger navigation/action icons, and larger SOS danger icons.

To run: double-click START_FISHER_SAFETY.bat. Do not open public/index.html directly. Keep the server window open while using the app.

Animations are visual explanations only. The numeric weather values come from the configured AccuWeather connection. No animation proves that conditions are safe.
